About

I am an M.S. student in Electrical Engineering and Computer Science at UC Irvine, where I work with Prof. Marco Levorato on edge AI and wireless systems. Before graduate school I was a software engineer at Oracle Financial Services Software.

My work sits between machine learning systems and the networks they run on: splitting inference across devices and edge servers, scheduling computation under unreliable wireless links, and measuring what actually happens on real radio hardware rather than in simulation alone.
